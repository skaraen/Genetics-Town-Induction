How The Land Works

Experience genetics education through immersive avatar creation and collaborative problem-solving

🧬 Step 1: DNA Selection

Begin by selecting random DNA sequences that determine your avatar's species. Each sequence codes for unique traits like bioluminescence in fireflies or echolocation in bats. Learn how just 0.1% genetic difference creates incredible diversity.

🎨 Step 2: Trait Customization

Fine-tune your avatar by adjusting gene expression levels. Turn genes on or off, modify protein production, and see real-time changes in your avatar's appearance and abilities. Discover how genetic regulation works in living organisms.

🌍 Step 3: World Exploration

Enter a shared virtual environment where your genetic creation comes to life. Visit themed areas like the DNA-to-RNA library, protein factory, and medicine restaurant. Each location teaches different aspects of molecular biology.

🤝 Step 4: Social Interaction

When avatars meet, they form memories through neural connection games. Connect neurons to strengthen synapses and learn how memories are physically formed in the brain through repeated neural firing patterns.

🚨 Step 5: Collaborative Crisis

Face a genetic virus that invades all avatars! Work together to identify the common viral sequence and develop ASO treatments. Experience how scientists research disease mechanisms and develop genetic therapies.

🏆 Step 6: Scientific Victory

Successfully cure the genetic bug through teamwork and scientific thinking. Learn about complementary base pairing, antisense oligonucleotides, and how modern genetic medicine works to treat diseases.
